He got pulled into machine learning early, and turned it into a habit of building. What began as tinkering has become first-author research, a slate of real internships, and a genuine obsession with turning data and markets into things that work.

By day he leads a student AI/ML education initiative now piloting district-wide with LAUSD. By night he runs first-author NLP research into how large language models perceive their own writing — work advised by a researcher at Google DeepMind.

Between the two, he interns across venture capital, edtech machine learning, and space-infrastructure research at a Caltech lab. The thread that runs through all of it is simple enough to print: find the pattern, build the model, ship the thing.

Asked what he is looking for, the answer is unglamorous — research, internships, and collaborators who take ideas seriously. The rest of this page is the evidence.
